Patient FAQ

Common Questions Answered

Are dental implants a durable long-term replacement for missing teeth? +

Yes. Biocompatible titanium implants fuse with your jawbone to prevent bone loss and support custom zirconia crowns that function like natural teeth for decades.

How long do Philips Zoom teeth whitening results last? +

With routine brushing and six-month cleanings, clinical Zoom whitening keeps your smile noticeably brighter for 12 to 24 months from a single appointment.
